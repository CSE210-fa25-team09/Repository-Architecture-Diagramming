## What Went Well 🌟
- Team Structure
  - As mentioned in the previous retrospective, the structure has worked out very effectively, streamlining our agile iterative workflow and produced very structured feedback for each subgroup and for the team as a whole.
- Leadership and ownership
  - Clear task distribution and responsibility allocation from the start meant every member owned their component and drove it forward without constant oversight.
  - Effective cross-team support across frontend, backend, and deployment kept coordination smooth and momentum strong.
- Strong Teamwork
  - Throughout the entirety of the project everyone was very communicative and capable of working collaboratively to share ideas and approaches to our main solutions.
  - We did an amazing job showcasing teamwork in our final group exam, splitting it accordingly and seeking reviews from the people we found to be the most suitable to speak on certain topics.

## What Didn't Go Well 🛑
- Attendance
  - This was an issue in previous sprints and remained one in these next two. It was hard to get everyone to be consistent as the workloads ramped up for everyone during the end of the quarter.
  - One positive was that some members started to show up when it was most necessary, and everyone was there for the Final group exam, in which we demonstrated strong teamwork.
- Documentation
  - We initially started off in a very volatile position and in a stage with little requirements and designs which forced us to adopt more documentation as a result. However, in this sprint that started to slow down and become an afterthought
  - Nonetheless we still were able to include our final ADRs but this occured more for a maintainability reason than iteration.

## What Could Be Improved For Future Teams 🚀
- Set deadlines and monitor blockers
  - Assign a clear deadline for each ticket and regularly check for blockers/conflicts so work doesn’t pile up at the end of the sprint.
- Mid-sprint and end-of-day touchpoints
  - Add brief mid-sprint check-ins to adjust scope early and avoid last-minute rushes.
  - During busy weeks, ask each member for a short end-of-day update to increase visibility and surface emerging blockers quickly.
